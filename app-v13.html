  • ・When money brings up a reaction
  • ・When a relationship stays on your mind
  • ・When work feels heavy
  • ・When the future feels uncertain
  • ・When you blame yourself
  • ・When you do not know what is happening inside

Noetune is not an app for analyzing your problems.

Through short questions, it helps you know yourself through what is happening now.
