# Noetune v17 AI Context

Use this document first when starting a new AI or Codex thread.

This is the current source of truth for Noetune v17 as of 2026-06-30.

---

# Most Important

Noetune is not a general SaaS product.

Do not design it from ordinary SaaS assumptions such as:

- save-based monetization
- AI-based monetization
- history-based monetization
- usage-limit monetization

Noetune must be designed from its own philosophy.

---

# What Noetune Is

Noetune is not a problem-solving app.

Noetune is not an AI coaching app.

Noetune is not a journaling app.

Noetune is a product for gradually lightening the user’s relationship with life.

The direction is not:

"I solved the problem."

The deeper direction is:

"What was the problem?"

This state should not be over-measured or over-explained.

---

# Core Philosophy

The user already has the answer.

Noetune does not provide answers.

Noetune creates a quiet structure where the user can notice through their own body, words, breath, and relationship with a theme.

AI analysis must not be added.

# Free

Free is complete.

Free users can complete the full v17 session.

Do not block any core step.

Do not add:

- session limits
- theme limits
- artificial usage limits
- partial-session paywalls

Free is not a teaser.

---

# Pro

Pro is not payment for saving.

Pro is not payment for history.

Pro is not payment for archives.

Pro is payment to continue the Journey.

---

# Journey

Journey is the central concept of v17.

A Journey is not stored data.

A Journey is the ongoing relationship between the user and one theme.

Examples:

- Money
- Love
- Family
- Health
- Business
- Existence

Some themes do not end in one session.

That is why Journey exists.

---

# Free Journey

In Free, each session is a new Journey.

When the session ends, the Journey ends.

The user may begin again from the start.

---

# Pro Journey

In Pro, Journeys are kept.

The user can continue from where they left off.

Examples:

- Money — Step 18
- Love — Step 5
- Work — Step 9
- Existence — Step 12

The value is not storage.

The value is continuity.

---

# Data Principle

Store only what is necessary to continue a Journey.

Recommended hierarchy:

User  
- Journey  
  - Session  
    - Loop  
      - Step

Do not build ordinary history browsing.

Do not build archive management.

Do not build note storage.
