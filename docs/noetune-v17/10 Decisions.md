# Noetune v17 Decisions

This document records major design decisions.

The purpose is not historical documentation.

It exists to preserve the reasoning behind important product choices.

---

# 2026-06-30

## Noetune Is Not a General SaaS

Decision:

Do not design from ordinary SaaS assumptions.

Reason:

Noetune is a philosophy-first product.

Every feature must originate from that philosophy.

---

## No AI Analysis

Decision:

AI analysis will not be implemented.

Reason:

The product exists to support the user's own noticing.

AI interpretation would replace part of that experience.

---

## Free Is Complete

Decision:

Free users experience the complete session.

Reason:

Noetune should never feel like a restricted trial.

One complete session should already create meaningful value.

---

## Pro Exists for Continuity

Decision:

Pro is not payment for storage.

Reason:

The value is continuing long-term relationships with life themes.

Storage is only a technical requirement.

Continuity is the product value.

---

## Journey Becomes the Core Object

Decision:

Journey replaces Session as the primary concept.

Reason:

Sessions are temporary.

Journeys continue across time.

The product should organize itself around relationships, not visits.

---

## Themes Are Entrances

Decision:

Themes are entrances into life, not content categories.

Reason:

Different themes often lead toward the same deeper questions.

Increasing themes increases possible Journeys.

---

## Save / History / Archive

Decision:

Avoid these concepts throughout the product.

Prefer:

- Journey
- Continue Journey
- Active Journeys

Reason:

The product should never feel like note management.

---

## Data Philosophy

Decision:

Store only what is required to continue.

Reason:

Accumulating data is not the goal.

Supporting continuity is.

---

## Founder Insight

Decision:

Past writing is not the primary value.

Reason:

The founder's own research filled more than ten notebooks.

They were eventually discarded.

The value had already moved into the next inquiry.

Noetune follows the same principle.

---

## Design Test

Every future feature must answer yes to these questions:

1. Does this deepen the experience of "What was the problem?"
2. Does this strengthen the value of continuing a Journey?
3. If this feature disappeared, would Noetune still work?

If the answer is no, the feature should not be added.

---

## Current Direction

The product is becoming:

- quieter
- simpler
- more relationship-centered
- less feature-centered

This direction should be preserved unless a future decision clearly improves the philosophy.
