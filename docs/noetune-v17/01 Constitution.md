# Noetune v17 Constitution

Date: 2026-06-30

## Core Principle

Noetune is not a general SaaS product.

It must not be designed from common SaaS assumptions such as:

- save-based monetization
- AI-based monetization
- history-based monetization
- usage-limit monetization

Every decision must be derived from the philosophy of Noetune.

## What Noetune Is

Noetune is not a problem-solving app.

It is not an app where AI gives answers.

Noetune is a product for gradually lightening one’s relationship with life.

The purpose is not to erase problems.

The deeper direction is to naturally approach the state of:

> What was the problem?

This state is not measured.

It is valuable because it cannot be fully measured.

## Product Goal

The goal of Noetune is not happiness 100%.

The goal is not finding the correct answer.

The goal is for the user’s relationship with a theme to change.

When that relationship changes deeply enough, the user may naturally feel:

> What was the problem?

## Theme

A theme is a direct question that quietly touches the truth.

Examples:

- Am I allowed to be as I am?
- Am I truly lovable?
- Can I truly live freely?
- Am I someone who can become abundant?
- Can I live as my original self?

Users may enter through life areas such as:

- Money
- Love
- Family
- Work
- Health
- Future
- Business
- SNS

Internally, these life areas may connect to deeper root questions.

Themes are not content quantity.

Themes are entrances into life.

The more themes Noetune has, the more entrances into Journey it offers.

## Core Flow

1. Select a theme
2. Measure the happiness score toward the theme only once at the beginning
3. Feel the 100% state
4. Write the current state
5. Measure that written state
6. Express it in different words
7. Breathe
8. Ask: What is the 100% me?
9. Breathe
10. Repeat steps 6–9 until no more answers arise
11. Re-measure
12. Repeat steps 3–11 until no more ideal states arise
13. Re-measure the original theme only at the end

This loop is the core of Noetune.

## Why No AI Analysis

Noetune does not use AI analysis.

Noetune is not a product where AI gives answers.

It is a product where the user notices through their own body.

AI analysis may damage the sacredness of the experience.

## Free

Free is not a trial version.

Free is the complete session experience.

Even one session must be enough for life to feel slightly lighter.

That is Noetune.

## Pro

Pro is not payment for saving.

Pro is not payment for history.

Pro is not payment for archives.

Pro is payment to continue the Journey.

## Journey

A Journey is not stored data.

A Journey is the ongoing relationship between a user and a theme.

Life contains many unfinished themes at the same time.

Examples:

- Money
- Love
- Family
- Health
- Business
- Existence

These do not always end in one session.

Therefore, they become Journeys.

## Free Journey

In Free, each session is a new Journey.

When it ends, it ends.

To begin again, the user begins from the start.

## Pro Journey

In Pro, Journeys are kept.

The user can continue from where they left off.

Examples:

- Money — Step 18
- Love — Step 5
- Work — Step 9
- Existence — Step 12

The value is not storage.

The value is continuity.

## Purpose of Keeping a Journey

The only purpose of keeping a Journey is:

> to continue from the previous point.

It is not for rereading the past.

It is not for memories.

It is not for analysis.

## Founder Insight

The founder wrote more than ten notebooks through self-healing research.

In the end, he did not want to reread them.

He threw them away.

The value was not in the past record.

The value was in the next inquiry.

Noetune is the same.

Noetune is not a product for looking back at the past.

It is a product for continuing the next step.

## Language

Avoid:

- Save
- History
- Archive

Prefer:

- Continue Journey
- Continue
- Journey
- Active Journeys

The value is not saving.

The value is continuing.

## Freedom to Let Go

Noetune is not a place to accumulate the past.

A Journey only needs to continue while it is alive.

When a Journey is complete, it may remain or it may be released.

The important thing is not the record.

The important thing is the user’s present relationship with life.

## Design Judgment Criteria

Before adding any new feature, ask:

1. Does this deepen the experience of “What was the problem?”, or does it disturb it?
2. Does this increase the value of continuing the Journey, or does it become mere data management?
3. If Noetune still works without this feature, is it truly necessary?

If a feature does not pass these three questions, it must not be added, even if it is common in ordinary SaaS products.

Noetune does not chase trends.

Noetune continues polishing one philosophy.
