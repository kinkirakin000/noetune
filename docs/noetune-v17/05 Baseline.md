# Noetune v17 Baseline

## Purpose

Baseline is not the destination.

It is the place from which the Journey begins.

Noetune does not attempt to define who the user is.

It simply observes where the relationship with a theme begins today.

---

## Why Measure First

Before every Journey, the user measures their present relationship with the chosen theme.

This measurement is not an evaluation.

It is a reference point.

Without a beginning, change cannot be quietly noticed.

---

## Observation, Not Judgment

A Baseline never means:

- good
- bad
- healthy
- unhealthy
- successful
- unsuccessful

It only represents the user's present relationship with a theme.

Nothing more.

---

## A Living Reference

The Baseline is temporary.

It may change tomorrow.

It may change during the next session.

It is not an identity.

---

## Theme-Specific

Every Journey has its own Baseline.

Examples:

- Money
- Love
- Family
- Health
- Business

Each relationship begins from a different place.

No universal Baseline exists.

---

## Beginning, Not Goal

The purpose of Baseline is not comparison.

It is orientation.

It quietly answers one question:

"Where am I beginning from today?"

---

## Design Principles

Baseline should always feel:

- simple
- quiet
- immediate
- non-judgmental

Users should complete it within a few seconds.

The Journey begins immediately afterward.

---

## Product Principle

If Baseline ever becomes a psychological test, personality assessment, or diagnostic tool, it has failed.

Its only purpose is to gently begin the Journey.
